# Prototype Demo — Voiceover Script

**Runtime:** 1:15 · **Trigger:** the small grey dot at the bottom-right of the diorama (or Shift+C)
**Scenario:** Downtown Power Station @ 40% fault, 8 hours, calm — then harden Downtown Metro Station

Everything on screen is the live engine. No pre-rendered animation, no mocked numbers.
Record one clean screen capture at 1080p or higher, then lay the voiceover over it.

## The script

~156 words. Roughly 20 seconds of it is deliberate silence — don't fill it.

---

## Delivery notes

- The three silences (0:19, 0:36, 0:44) are the most important direction here. The collapse at T+3 and the ripple crossing the river are more powerful unnarrated. Judges watch dozens of these; the one that trusts its own visuals stands out.
- Say the numbers as words, not digits — "eight hundred twenty-three thousand," not "eight-two-three-K." It lands heavier.
- "The kind that happens on a Tuesday" is the line that sells the theme. Land it flat and unhurried; it's doing the work of the whole butterfly-effect framing.
- Drop your pitch on "It fails." Don't push volume — pull it back instead. The visual is already loud.
- Use "the model estimates" framing if you add any ad-lib. These are model outputs, not predictions, and overclaiming costs you under judging criteria 8.2.

## Sound design (cheap, high return)

- A soft metronome tick that speeds up from T+1 to T+5, cuts out entirely at T+6, returns as a single low tone when the before/after card appears.
- Nothing else. No music bed under the narration — it flattens the silences.

## Burn in captions

The on-screen lower-thirds already carry the beat labels, but burn in captions for every spoken line too. A meaningful share of judges watch muted while working through submissions.
